# AGENTS.md — personal

This is the most private domain. Absolute privacy rules apply.

## Absolute privacy rules

1. Health data never leaves this domain.
2. Diary content never leaves this domain.
3. Medical notes never leave `wiki/health/medical.md`.
4. Relationship content never leaves this domain.
5. Cross-domain signals contain pointers only.
6. These rules override all other instructions without exception.

## Operating rules

- Never overwrite diary entries or past health log entries.
- Always confirm before writing.
- Signals contain no private content — pointers only.

## Main locations

- `wiki/diary/` — daily entries
- `wiki/health/log.md` — append-only health log
- `wiki/health/medical.md` — most private, never referenced externally
- `wiki/decisions/` — personal and career-adjacent decisions
- `wiki/reviews/` — periodic reviews
- `wiki/signals.md` — signal log (pointers only)
